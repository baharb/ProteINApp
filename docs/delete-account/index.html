Delete your ProteIN account

Last updated: 2026-10-14

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ProteIN and sign in.
  2. Tap your profile picture (top right) to open Profile.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

Email the contact email shown on our Google Play / App Store listing from the address you signed up with and ask us to delete your account. We'll do it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your login, profile (name, photo, age, height, weight, goals), logged meals and weight history — permanently. Meal photos and voice recordings are never stored, so there is nothing else to delete.
