ProteIN Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

ProteIN helps you reach a daily protein goal. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and your choices. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Data we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password (handled by our login provider, Supabase; we never see your password).
  • Profile: optional name and photo, and the details you enter to calculate your protein target — age, gender, height, weight, activity level and goal.
  • Meals: the meals you log (name, protein, calories, time of day), so you can see your progress and streak.
  • Meal photos: when you scan a meal, the photo is sent to our server to identify the food. It is processed and then discarded — we do not store meal photos.
  • Voice: when you use the microphone, the recording is sent to our server to turn it into text, then deleted. We do not keep recordings.
  • Text you type to describe a meal or ask the coach, used only to answer you.

Guests (not signed in) can browse the app; nothing is sent to our server for guests.

How we use it

  • To calculate your protein target, show your progress, streak and meal suggestions.
  • To identify food in photos and text, and to answer coach questions, using an AI service.
  • To limit daily use per account so the service stays available and free.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — stores your account, profile and logged meals.
  • Groq — AI processing of meal photos, meal descriptions, voice and coach messages. Content is sent only to produce your answer.
  • Render — hosts our server.

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Health information

ProteIN gives general nutrition information, not medical advice. Talk to a qualified professional before making health decisions.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account and all its data at any time: in the app go to Profile → Account → Delete account, or see how to delete your account. Deletion is permanent.

Children

ProteIN is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or requests: the contact email shown on our Google Play / App Store listing.

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
