ProteIN Help

Log a meal

  • Type or say it: on Today, write what you ate in any language (e.g. "2 eggs and toast") or tap 🎤 and speak, tap 🎤 again to stop.
  • Scan it: tap the green Scan button, then Take photo. To leave the camera without a photo, press your phone's Back button.
  • Your foods: foods you log are saved — tap one to log it again and choose how much.

Numbers look wrong?

Estimates can be off. Remove a meal with ✕ in Today's log and add it again with the amount (e.g. "2 chicken breasts").

"You've used today's free limit"

The free version has a daily limit for scans, voice and the coach. It resets the next day; typing a meal and Your foods still work.

Change language or target

Open Profile (your picture, top corner) → Language, or Edit profile to update your weight and goal — your protein target updates automatically.

Delete your account

Profile → Account → Delete account. More info.
